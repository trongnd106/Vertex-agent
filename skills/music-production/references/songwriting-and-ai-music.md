# Songwriting & AI Music Generation

Everything here is a GUIDELINE, not a rule. Art breaks rules on purpose.

## Song Structures
- **ABABCB** — Verse/Chorus/Verse/Chorus/Bridge/Chorus (most pop/rock)
- **AABA** — Verse/Verse/Bridge/Verse (refrain-based, jazz/ballads)
- **ABAB** — Verse/Chorus alternating (simple, direct)
- **AAA** — Strophic, no chorus (folk, storytelling)

Song building blocks: Intro → Verse (story) → Pre-Chorus (tension) → Chorus (emotional core) → Bridge (shift) → Outro

## AI Music Prompts (Suno, etc.)

Effective prompts include: genre, mood, tempo, instrumentation, vocal style, structure hints.

**Template:**
```
[Genre tags] [Mood descriptors] [Tempo/BPM]
"Instruments: [instrument list]"
"Vocals: [vocal character]"
[Structure notes]
---
[Lyrics]
---
```n
**Example prompt:**
```
Synthwave, retro, driving, cinematic
BPM: 128
Instruments: arpeggiated synths, gated reverb snare, bass pad
Vocals: breathy female, reverb-drenched
Structure: intro 8 bars → verse 16 → chorus 16 → bridge 8 → chorus out
---
[Chorus]
Under neon skies we ride
...
```

## Lyric Writing Tips
- Show, don't tell — use concrete imagery
- Vary line lengths for rhythmic interest
- Use internal rhyme for flow
- Consider meter — each line should feel natural when sung
